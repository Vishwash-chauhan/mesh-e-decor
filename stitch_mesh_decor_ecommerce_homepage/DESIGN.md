## Brand & Style
The brand embodies heirloom artisanal craftsmanship, high-end bespoke metalwork, and premier corporate gifting. It operates at the intersection of traditional silversmithing, warm polished brass, and contemporary luxury interior architecture. The target audience comprises discerning interior designers, luxury homeowners, luxury event curators, and enterprise procurement directors seeking memorable corporate gifts.

The aesthetic philosophy is an elevated, warm luxury editorial style:
- **Warm Architectural Minimalism:** Built upon tactile, warm ivory surfaces rather than stark digital whites, evoking parchment, travertine, and fine handmade paper.
- **Museum-Grade Visual Restraint:** Ample negative space frames silver and brass artifacts with intentional focal pacing.
- **Sharpened Editorial Typography:** High-contrast serif headlines evoke heritage catalogues and luxury monographs, balanced by crisp, rhythmic geometric sans-serif details.
- **Jeweled Signature Accents:** Grounded in deep obsidian with calibrated golden-brass and vivid scarlet crimson puncturing through at moments of decisive action.

## Colors
The color palette draws directly from the physical materials of the atelier: silver plate, hand-cast brass, dark slate displays, and the heritage seal.

- **Primary (`#121212` Obsidian Charcoal):** Used for primary typography, authoritative framing, structured architectural borders, and dominant CTA buttons.
- **Secondary (`#C81E27` Imperial Crimson):** Sourced from the triangular apex of the brand mark. Reserved strictly for intentional visual anchors: active selection indicators, VIP badges, exclusive collection highlights, and critical conversion triggers.
- **Tertiary (`#B89047` Aged Brass / Artisan Gold):** Inspired by polished brass finishes and warm metallic reflections. Applied to hairline rules, decorative dividers, subtle badge backdrops, and secondary interactive states.
- **Neutral Base (`#FAF7F2` Alabaster Parchment):** The foundation canvas that softens eye strain and imparts an editorial, physical paper tactile warmth. Complemented by `#F3ECE2` (Warm Travertine) for cards, alternating product bands, and container surfaces.
- **Surface Contrast Tier:** `#E8DEC8` serves as a muted border and subtle inset divider, maintaining organic warmth across all interactive components without cold grey cast shadows.

## Typography
The typographic architecture pairs the classical literary cadence of **Playfair Display** with the clean, legible clarity of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

- **Editorial Headlines (Playfair Display):** Conveys the poise of exhibition catalogues and luxury private sales. Headings utilize generous line spacing and natural italics for dates, collections, or artisanal descriptions.
- **Functional Body & Metadata (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Selected for its gentle humanist curves and geometric construction. It ensures high legibility for item SKUs, dimensional specifications, pricing schedules, and wholesale terms.
- **Tracked Monograph Labels:** Category tags, item codes (e.g., `ITEM: JGM-0076`), and tier banners are set in uppercase with deliberate letter spacing (`0.14em` to `0.22em`) to echo high-end engraving plates.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a fluid 12-column grid system bounded by a maximum container width of `1440px`, prioritizing gallery-style presentation:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` outer page margins. Section vertical spacing expands to `space-xl` (`3.5rem` to `5rem`) to create restful pacing between collections.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns with `1.25rem` gutters and `1.5rem` outer canvas padding. Split catalogue layouts switch to a 2-column stacked sequence.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1rem` margins. Complex editorial splits transform into clean single-column cards with edge-to-edge photography.
- **Rhythm & Whitespace:** Whitespace is treated as a structural luxury element. Product imagery is never crowded; items must breathe within generous neutral framing (`#FAF7F2`), mirroring the physical presentation in an art gallery.

## Elevation & Depth
This design system avoids heavy blurred drop shadows or neon blurs, favoring tactile editorial realism through architectural layering:

- **Surface Tiers:** Depth is built by layering surfaces. The base canvas `#FAF7F2` supports elevated cards and panels finished in `#F3ECE2` or pure `#FFFFFF`, outlined by `#E8DEC8`.
- **Low-Contrast Hairline Outlines:** Card boundaries, table divisions, and specimen frames use crisp 1px borders in `#E8DEC8` or semi-transparent charcoal (`rgba(18, 18, 18, 0.08)`).
- **Subtle Ambient Shadows:** In floating drawers, bespoke enquiry modals, and sticky top headers, elevation is achieved via diffused, warm-tinted ambient shadows: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(45, 38, 28, 0.07), 0 4px 12px rgba(45, 38, 28, 0.03)`.
- **Metallic Edge Lighting:** Selected brass cards or featured corporate packaging cards feature an inset hairline highlight: `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)` to reflect light like polished silverware.

## Shapes
The shape philosophy adheres to **0 (Sharp)** geometric precision. 

- **Architectural Edge:** Buttons, image containers, input fields, badges, and modal dialogs utilize precise 90-degree squared corners (`border-radius: 0px`). This honors the linear structure of silversmith drafting grids, metallic hallmarks, and architectural display plinths.
- **Bespoke Framing Accents:** Fine rectilinear borders (0.5px to 1px) frame primary assets. Corner accents and subtle center-aligned hairline dividing rules bring a bespoke, monogrammed identity to the overall interface.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Action (Obsidian Solid):** Crisp black (`#121212`) background, ivory text (`#FAF7F2`), sharp corners, tracked uppercase typography (`label-lg`). Hover states transition to warm brass `#B89047` with smooth cubic-bezier timing.
- **Secondary Action (Hairline Brass / Obsidian Border):** 1px solid `#121212` border with transparent background, shifting to a solid fill on hover with inverted text.
- **Crimson VIP / Enquire CTA:** Crimson (`#C81E27`) fill with white text, dedicated exclusively to wholesale orders, catalog downloads, and urgent corporate sample requests.
- **Text Buttons:** High-contrast underline positioned 4px below text with a 1px brass or crimson rule that expands horizontally upon focus and hover.

### Product & Specimen Cards
- **Editorial Split Cards:** Two-tone horizontal or vertical cards combining high-resolution artifact photography with warm travertine (`#F3ECE2`) metadata panes. 
- **Typography Layout:** Card headers display the uppercase SKU (e.g., `ITEM: JGM-0076`) in `label-md` alongside currency values in `headline-md` Playfair Display.
- **Ornamental Motif Dividers:** Subtle heritage filigree lines or minimal brass hairline rules separate the product description from commercial volume pricing tiers.

### Input Fields & RFQ Forms (Request for Quote)
- **Text Fields:** Minimalist under-line or fully enclosed 1px borders in `#E8DEC8`. Background fills rest on pure white or `#FAF7F2`. Focused fields darken to `#121212` with a floating uppercase label.
- **Dropdown & Selectors:** Custom architectural drawers with brass indicator carets and generous padding (`space-md`) for effortless bulk order quantity selection.

### Chips, Tags & Status Badges
- **Specification Badges:** Flat, rectangular tags with 1px border. Brass badges feature `#F3ECE2` fill with `#B89047` typography and border for material callouts (e.g., "SILVER-PLATED", "SOLID BRASS").
- **Wholesale Tier Indicators:** Crimson accent tags with `#C81E27` solid fills or 10% opacity tints for "CORPORATE EXCLUSIVE" or "BESPOKE RUN".

### Selection Controls
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Unyielding square profiles with 1.5px charcoal borders. Active state renders a solid crimson or obsidian fill with an interior ivory icon.

### Bespoke Quotation & Wholesale Drawer
- **Gifting Ingot Summary:** Fixed side-sheet overlaying the catalogue, designed with editorial typography, allowing corporate buyers to curate specimen sets, inspect tiered packaging finishes, and directly submit RFQ dossiers.
